# Personal-Brand — Section inventory (in order)

10 sections between nav and footer. Deep per-section specs live in
`sections/*.md` — this file is the composition overview.

## Above-the-fold

1. **Nav** — card-dropdown overlay. Dark, floating with backdrop-blur.
   Logo left, product/course links center, "Join" or "Enroll" CTA
   right. Dropdown menu opens as an overlay card on click.
   (See `sections/nav.md` for detailed spec.)
2. **Hero (dark portrait split)** — copy on left (headline + subhead
   + trust row + dual CTA), portrait photo on right bleeding to edge.
   Dark stage + dot-grid bg. Namecard for the founder inline below
   the portrait.
   (See `sections/hero.md`.)

## The bento

3. **MegaBento (6-tile grid)** — 6-column grid, 3 rows of mixed-size
   tiles. Image tile + gradient tile + review tile + benefit tile +
   image tile + CTA tile — pattern varies. This is the visual signature
   of the reference.
   (See `sections/mega-bento.md`.)

## Lead magnet + community

4. **Watchlist (lead-magnet split-form)** — split section. Subscribe
   form on one side (email input + submit), preview image of the
   lead magnet on the other. "Free — get the [thing]."
   (See `sections/watchlist.md`.)
5. **Community (screenshot-benefits)** — Discord/Slack/Circle
   screenshot on LEFT, numbered benefits list on RIGHT. "Inside the
   community, you'll get..."
   (See `sections/community.md`.)

## Value proposition + CTA

6. **HelpGrid (3-column icon)** — centered heading + 3 icon-cards.
   Each: stroke icon (16-20px) + benefit headline + short prose.
   No decorative icons; content-signal icons only.
   (See `sections/help-grid.md`.)
7. **Spotlight (full-bleed CTA)** — full-bleed section with a
   single centered CTA. Optional GridDistortion or animated bg for
   the "wow" moment. Use sparingly.
   (See `sections/spotlight.md`.)

## Long-form: welcome + reviews

8. **HeroSplit (welcome bento)** — media LEFT (video embed + quote
   + photo bento), copy RIGHT (welcome message + secondary CTAs).
   Second-act hero after the initial hero + bento.
   (See `sections/hero-split.md`.)
9. **Reviews (video + wall)** — 3 vertical video reels TOP + LinkedIn
   screenshot masonry BOTTOM. Real testimonial content, not stock.
   (See `sections/reviews.md`.)

## Close: social + founder story

10. **YoutubeCta (dark card with reel)** — copy LEFT + gradient card
    with embedded video reel RIGHT. "Watch me on YouTube" or the
    creator's primary platform.
    (See `sections/youtube-cta.md`.)
11. **AboutFounder (long-form founder split)** — large portrait photo
    LEFT + 5-paragraph story RIGHT. The human hook at the end of
    the page.
    (See `sections/about-founder.md`.)
12. **Footer** — multi-column with legal + social + copyright
    (CURRENT year).

## Adaptation rules

For coaches (single-person, no course product yet):

- Drop MegaBento (3) if the creator has few visual assets — replace
  with a testimonial band
- Simplify Watchlist (4) — replace with a booking CTA if there's no
  lead magnet yet
- Elevate AboutFounder (11) — expand to hero-level prominence

For course creators launching a NEW cohort:

- Elevate Spotlight (7) — this becomes the enrollment CTA moment
- Add pricing section between Spotlight (7) and HeroSplit (8) if
  there's a paid product
- Community (5) becomes central: "Enrolled = access to this"

For established creators with a big audience:

- Keep all 10 sections; this reference was built for exactly this
  case
- Consider adding a "As seen in" logo row after Hero IF the logos
  are real (podcast appearances, publications) — never fake

## When personal-brand is picked as SECONDARY (paired reference)

Only these signature moves get remixed into the primary:
- Bento tile idea (if primary is a boxy reference)
- Dark stage aesthetic (only if primary is also dark)
- Video-reel review pattern
- Portrait-split hero move

Skip: the exact 10-section flow, the deep spec files, GridDistortion.
