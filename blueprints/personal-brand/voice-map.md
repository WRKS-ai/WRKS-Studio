# Personal-Brand — Voice map

Which users this reference is the RIGHT remix source for.

## Primary fit

- **voice_descriptor**: `bold`, `warm`, `expert`
- **business_type**: `personal_brand`
- **primary_goal**: `build_audience`, `sell_products`, `book_calls`,
  `capture_leads`
- **site_intent**: any

Best for: creators, coaches, course-sellers, community builders,
newsletter authors selling a product, YouTubers with a course,
educators with a paid cohort, freelancers building a personal brand,
solopreneurs with an audience, indie hackers with a product,
consultants with a signature methodology, keynote speakers,
authors selling a book / cohort.

## Secondary fit (usable with adaptation)

- **voice_descriptor**: `professional` — works if the creator is a
  professional-service adjacent creator (executive coach, financial
  educator). Soften the dark canvas one notch.
- **business_type**: `service` (specifically consulting) — works when
  the consultant IS the brand (not a team). Boutique consultancies
  led by a named founder.
- **business_type**: `agency` (small agency, single founder) — works
  for founder-led agencies where the founder's personal brand
  matters more than the agency name.

## Do NOT use for

- `voice_descriptor: quiet` — the whole aesthetic is loud + confident;
  quiet voices will feel muffled. Use Tomorrow's Sunrise instead.
- `voice_descriptor: playful` — leans confident, not playful. If we
  need playful, Bill-Fanter can bend but a purpose-built playful
  reference would be better.
- `business_type: saas` (product company) — the personal-brand
  aesthetic clashes with product-forward SaaS positioning; use WRKS
  Online.
- `business_type: ecommerce` — needs product-imagery grid, not
  founder-forward personality.
- Law, medical, dentistry — trust signals expected in professional
  services don't fit; use Claxton Law.
- Wellness communities, nonprofits — use Tomorrow's Sunrise.

## Pair with

Can be paired with:
- **WRKS Online** — when creator has a very product-forward offering
  (a tool, an app, a paid platform). Draws creator personality from
  personal-brand + product-mockup depth from WRKS Online.

Never pair with:
- **Tomorrow's Sunrise** — dark vs cream palette clash; the two
  vibes don't share vocabulary
- **Claxton Law** — creator informality clashes with
  professional-service formality

## Signal strength

If the user's brand ingest reveals **dark palette + portrait
photography of a single founder + testimonials from named students
or clients**, this reference is the automatic first pick. Weight it
80/20 against secondary references.
