# Claxton Law — Voice map

Which users this reference is the RIGHT remix source for.

## Primary fit

- **voice_descriptor**: `professional`, `expert`
- **business_type**: `service` (professional service specifically)
- **primary_goal**: `book_calls`, `capture_leads`
- **site_intent**: any

Best for: law firms, medical practices, dental practices, chiropractic
clinics, veterinary practices, accounting firms, financial advisors,
insurance agents, real-estate agents, architecture firms, consulting
firms, therapy practices (individual + group), notary services,
tax preparation, immigration services, licensed contractors, home
services (plumbing/HVAC/electrical when they want to elevate).

## Secondary fit (usable with adaptation)

- **voice_descriptor**: `warm` — works for warm-professional
  practitioners (family lawyer, community-clinic doctor). Soften the
  navy accent to a warm tone.
- **voice_descriptor**: `bold` — works for aggressive practices
  (personal injury attorneys, criminal defense). Sharpen the copy
  tone and add stronger CTAs.
- **business_type**: `agency` (creative/brand agency) — works if the
  agency wants trust-signal-heavy positioning. Skip the pricing
  section, keep the team + case sections.

## Do NOT use for

- `voice_descriptor: playful` — the whole aesthetic is deliberately
  serious; playful voices will feel wrong
- `business_type: saas` (any tech product) — needs product-forward
  design; use WRKS Online
- `business_type: ecommerce` — needs product-imagery grid; use
  future ecommerce reference
- `business_type: personal_brand` (creator / coach / influencer) —
  needs personality-forward design; use Bill-Fanter
- Wellness communities, nonprofits, memberships — use Tomorrow's
  Sunrise
- Modern SaaS agencies or performance-marketing shops — use WRKS
  Online

## Pair with

Can be paired with:
- **Tomorrow's Sunrise** — when user is a warm-but-professional
  practitioner (family therapist, wellness clinic, holistic
  doctor). Draws trust signals from Claxton + warm imagery from
  Tomorrow's Sunrise.
- **WRKS Online** — when user is an elite consulting firm (management
  consultants, boutique strategy shops). Draws trust density from
  Claxton + case-study depth from WRKS Online.

Never pair with:
- **Bill-Fanter** — creator/personal-brand aesthetics clash with
  professional-service trust signals

## Signal strength

If the user's brand ingest reveals **light bg + navy/blue accent +
credentials / certifications / association logos on their current
site**, this reference is the automatic first pick. Weight it 75/25
against secondary references.
