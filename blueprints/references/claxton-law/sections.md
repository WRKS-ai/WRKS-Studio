# Claxton Law — Section inventory (in order)

~14 sections between nav and footer. Tightest of the four references
— professional services needs LESS not more.

## Above-the-fold

1. **Nav** — sticky horizontal bar, light. Logo left, service links
   center (dropdown for practice areas), location links + contact CTA
   right. Language switcher if applicable.
2. **Hero** — full-bleed light canvas. Direct question or promise as
   headline ("Do You Need Help with Immigration?" — or the user's
   equivalent, keep it plainspoken). Subhead explains what you offer.
   Two CTAs (primary phone/whatsapp/booking + secondary email).
   Working hours visible near the CTA.

## Immediate trust

3. **Trust signals row** — centered, single row. Icon or number +
   short label pattern: "20+ Years Experience · 500+ Cases Handled ·
   98% Client Satisfaction · 4 Practice Areas · [Professional
   association logos]". Hairline-separated cells, restrained.
4. **Services grid / carousel** — 9-column carousel OR 3×3 grid.
   Each service card: icon (16-20px, stroke) + service title (all-caps
   overline optional) + 1-line description. Click → service detail
   page.

## Social proof + case validation

5. **Reviews carousel** — client testimonials with 5-star rating +
   reviewer name + practice area context. Not photo-heavy — quote
   and name are the point.
6. **Recent case wins** — minimal single-case highlight. Client name
   (if consented) or "Recent Client" + case type badge + outcome +
   one-line detail. Rotating carousel or static.

## The people

7. **Team / attorney bios** — 5-column carousel that collapses to
   single-column on scroll. Each: headshot (real photo, warm
   background), name, title, credentials line, 3-4 sentence bio.
   Include personal detail when it exists (background, languages,
   community involvement).

## The commercial layer

8. **Consultation pricing** — 2-column split. Column 1: "Online
   consultation · 30 minutes · $X" + CTA (Book Online). Column 2:
   "In-person consultation · 1 hour · $Y" + CTA (Schedule). Equal
   visual weight; user picks the fit.

## The brand narrative

9. **About / mission** — split section. Text-left (headline,
   descriptive paragraph, firm history stats: "Founded 2005", "20+
   years combined experience", "3 offices") + image-right (office
   interior, team candid, or founder portrait).

## Content / education

10. **Guides / resources** — text-grid, link-heavy. 6-8 guide titles
    + short descriptions + "View All" CTA. Warm background. This is
    lead-nurturing content, not selling.
11. **Firm history callout** — centered narrow callout. "20+ Years"
    as big number, bullet points below (licensed in X states, member
    of AILA, languages spoken, etc.). Brief.
12. **Blog teasers** — 2 or 3-column grid. Each: featured image +
    category tag (mono caps) + headline + date. Recent posts only,
    3-6 max.

## Lead capture + footer

13. **Contact form** — single-column form. Name, phone, email,
    message, privacy checkbox, submit button. Above form: brief
    "Send us a message" heading. Below form: alternative contact
    (email + phone with international format).
14. **Footer** — multi-column stacked. Logo, email signup, service
    links, hours, office addresses with full mailing addresses,
    socials, legal links (Privacy, Terms, Accessibility, Disclaimer).
    Copyright CURRENT year.

## Adaptation rules

For solo practitioners (single lawyer, therapist, accountant):

- Drop team carousel (7) — replace with single about-founder split
- Drop consultation pricing (8) if pricing varies per case (replace
  with "Book a free 20-minute consult" CTA)
- Drop firm-history callout (11) — combine into about section
- Keep: hero, trust signals row, services, reviews, about, guides,
  contact form, footer. ~9 sections minimum.

For medical / dental practices:

- Replace "practice areas" with "conditions treated" or "services"
- Add insurance-accepted section between services (4) and reviews (5)
- Add appointment-booking widget integration on hero CTA
- Everything else translates directly

For financial advisors / accountants:

- Add certifications visibly (CPA, CFP, RIA logos) in trust signals row
- Add "regulated by" language in footer legal
- Everything else translates directly

For consulting firms (management / strategy):

- Drop consultation-pricing 2-col (8) — replace with case studies
  grid (borrow from WRKS Online reference)
- Elevate team bios (7) — expand to full sections per partner
- Add "industries served" grid between services and reviews
