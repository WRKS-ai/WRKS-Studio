# WRKS Online — Character

Source: [wrksonline.com](https://wrksonline.com) — the WRKS agency's own
marketing site. Reference bundle for **dark, confident, data-heavy
SaaS + agency + performance-marketing** positioning.

## The vibe in one line

Framer × Linear hybrid, layered with performance-marketing specificity.
Dark, minimalist, confident. Real product screenshots and data
visualization stand in for abstract feature copy. Reads as *authoritative
but approachable* — sales-focused without being aggressive.

## Signature moves

- **Dark canvas throughout** — charcoal/near-black bg, warm white ink,
  one electric-blue accent used sparingly for CTAs + status indicators
- **Italic-accented headlines** — one italicized noun per hero
  ("AI growth marketing for _your business_"). Creates visual rhythm
  without underline. Restrained, one word per line max.
- **Real screenshots over illustration** — Meta Ads Manager, funnels,
  agent-build sessions, budget charts. Never abstract UI mockups.
- **Data pairing** — every claim sits next to a metric or a chart.
  "500+ businesses served" beside "Over $20M ad spend managed."
- **Named framework** — a proprietary methodology gets its own section
  (BLAS = Build · Launch · Adapt · Scale) with tabbed detail per phase.
- **Asymmetric splits** — text-left / visuals-right (or reversed) at
  ~60/40 width. Never centered-and-symmetrical for deep sections.
- **Editorial constraint on prose width** — body text capped ~600px
  even on wide viewports. Readability > spread.
- **Dual-CTA endings** — every major section ends with a primary +
  secondary CTA close together (call + case-study).
- **Multi-layer proof** — trust logos → case-study cards → stats
  banner → team → FAQ. Objections handled from multiple angles.

## What NOT to steal from this reference

- **Italics** — DESIGN.md bans italics globally. WRKS Online breaks
  its own rule here; when we remix, replace italic accents with
  weight-contrast or color-contrast, not `<em>`.
- **BLAS framework** — that's a WRKS-specific IP, not a generic
  pattern. When remixing, replace with the user's own methodology if
  they have one, or drop the section entirely.

## Tone character (adjective cluster)

Confident. Restrained. Data-forward. Sales-conscious. Editorial.
Never cheerful. Never soft. Never ornamental.

## Density profile

Airy at the outer rhythm (100-120px between sections) + dense inside
each section (mockups, charts, and tight-tracked headlines pack the
frame). Not sparse. Not cluttered.

## Reference cluster this belongs to

Framer (dark), Linear (dark editorial), Vercel (dark restrained),
Mercury (financial confidence). Not: Aesop, Notion Calendar,
Basecamp.
