# Tomorrow's Sunrise — Section inventory (in order)

~19 sections between nav and footer. Use as inspiration, not literal
sequence — this many sections works for a paid-community launch site;
adapt for smaller businesses.

## Above-the-fold

1. **Nav** — sticky horizontal bar, light-cream. Logo left, sparse
   nav links center, join CTA right. NO glassmorphism (this palette
   doesn't want it).
2. **Hero** — full-bleed carousel of soft-focus nature imagery
   (sunrises, florals, skies) rotating slowly. Large emotional
   headline overlaid ("You don't have to do this alone" — the
   REAL user's version). One quiet CTA below. NEVER add gradient
   overlay to the images; let them breathe.

## The brand statement

3. **Brand statement** — three centered paragraphs with section
   labels above each (`Loss`, `Family`, `Community` — or the user's
   equivalent domain words). Long-form editorial prose. Body text
   at ~19px, 1.7 line-height, capped at ~640px width.

## Optional media

4. **Founder video** — embedded player centered, ~1160px wide.
   Play controls only, no autoplay. Warm caption below.

## The framework

5. **Four pillars** — 4-column grid, equal cards, cream bg. Each:
   numbered label (`:01 Pillar`), bold header, short prose. No
   icons — text and structure alone. Compact rhythm inside cards,
   airy between them.

## Community narrative

6. **Community overview** — full-width, centered prose. Two long
   paragraphs. Soft floral background image at ~15% opacity behind.
   Two CTAs (primary + tertiary link).

## Feature spotlights (alternating image-text pairs)

7-10. **Four feature spotlights** — each = image-text pair.
   - Section 7: image-LEFT + text-RIGHT
   - Section 8: image-RIGHT + text-LEFT
   - Section 9: image-LEFT + text-RIGHT
   - Section 10: image-RIGHT + text-LEFT
   Each has: soft-focus photo (500-700px wide), headline, bulleted
   3-5 feature list, one "Explore" CTA. Generous 88-100px vertical
   padding between them.

## The onboarding journey

11. **Onboarding approach** — left-aligned prose with numbered steps
    (`01. Personal setup`, `02. Welcome call`, `03. First small step`)
    and descriptive text under each. Background floral image at low
    opacity. Ends with a narrative wrap-up paragraph.

## Membership / paid offering

12. **Membership intro** — full-width centered prose. Two paragraphs
    justifying the paid model. Emphasis on sustainability + intention,
    never on urgency or scarcity.
13. **Membership tiers** — 3-column price cards, stacked vertically.
    Monthly / Quarterly / Yearly. Long feature list per card in
    expandable detail. NO "MOST POPULAR" badge. NO gradient card
    borders. Restrained.

## First-week emotional CTA

14. **First-week promises** — 4-card grid. Small icons OR photos,
    numbered labels (`:01` – `:04`), short emotional promises per
    card. "In your first week you'll..." — direct address, second
    person, warm.

## Social proof

15. **Testimonials carousel** — full-width. Warm gradient background
    (peach → beige → gray). Long-form quote (100-200 words visible,
    "Read Full Testimonial" opens modal with 300-500 word full text).
    Reviewer name + location, small photo optional.

## Objection handling

16. **FAQ section** — accordion, centered, 8-12 questions with
    detailed answers (not one-liners). Clean typography, generous
    padding inside the accordion.

## Close

17. **Final CTA banner** — centered full-width. Cream bg. Headline +
    subhead + single CTA button. Quiet, not loud.
18. **Footer** — multi-column grid. Links (Explore, Spotlight,
    Contact), socials, app links, copyright, legal links. Copyright
    CURRENT year.

## Adaptation rules

For smaller wellness/coaching businesses (not paid community):

- Drop membership tiers (13) if there's no paid offering
- Drop membership intro (12) if there's no membership at all
- Collapse feature spotlights from 4 → 2 (7-10 becomes 7-8)
- Keep: hero carousel, brand statement, 2 pillars, 2 spotlights,
  onboarding approach, 3-5 testimonials, 6-8 FAQ, final CTA, footer

For nonprofits:

- Replace membership tiers with donation tiers (same shape)
- Add impact stats section (numbers + narrative)
- Keep the emotional emphasis and long-form testimonials

For yoga studios, therapists, life coaches (individual practitioners):

- Drop community overview (single-practitioner, not community)
- Add class schedule / booking calendar section between spotlights
  and testimonials
- Drop first-week promises grid (14) — too community-focused
